# Roadmap

Things we have decided to build but have not yet. (What exists today is described in the README.)

## From the latest round of feedback

- **Force an answer before assigning.** When a task touches a kink the other person has not answered, the app
  makes _them_ answer it when the dom tries to assign - so nothing is ever assigned on a blank.
- **The sub chooses what the dom must fill in.** In their profile, the sub can list which of **body, kinks, rules
  and what's in bounds** must be filled in by the dom before the dom can assign them a task.
- **In-site notifications.** New tasks, new or changed rules, comments, pauses and endings, shown inside the app.
- **Empty rules prompt.** If a dynamic has no rules, show the dom a clear button prompting them to write some.
  Its confirm step should remind them to discuss the rules with their partner first.

## Still to come

- Recurring tasks (due dates and a repeat rule).
- Role swaps - permanent and time-limited - with both people agreeing.
- Photo / video / audio and location proof for tasks (needs file storage and location consent).
- The **Items** and **Spaces** lists for "What's in bounds?" (Items is the old equipment list for now; Spaces is
  empty, so its section stays hidden).
- Decide what **My Body** should hold now that "What's in bounds?" also covers anatomy.
- Deploying to Cloudflare (needs a Cloudflare login).
